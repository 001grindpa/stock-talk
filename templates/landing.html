How Stocktalk Works

Four simple steps to non-custodial natural-language trading.

01

Connect Base Wallet

Connect your injected Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.). Full support for Basename and ENS profiles.

02

Type Your Intent

Describe what you want to achieve, e.g. “swap $2 USDC for AAPL”, “buy $5 each of AAPL MSFT GOOGL”, or “gift 0.01 AMZN to name.base.eth”.

03

Agent Builds Transaction Card

Stocktalk returns an unsigned swap card, multi-leg basket card, or gift card. Complete any needed fields directly in chat.

04

Review & Wallet Sign

Review details and confirm each transaction in your wallet. The server never holds private keys, signs transactions, or custodians gifts.
